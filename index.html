Israeli Radio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Israeli Radio ("the app") is an Android TV app that streams live Israeli radio stations. This page explains what data the app does and does not handle.

No account, no sign-in

The app has no login, no user accounts, and no registration of any kind. Nothing you enter identifies you to us, because there is nothing to enter.

What stays on your device

The app remembers which stations you've played, so your most recently played station appears first and can resume automatically on launch. This play history is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere, never leaves your device, and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What the app sends over the network

To play a station, your device connects directly to that station's own streaming servers (for example, each station's own CDN) to receive the live audio, and may load that station's logo image from the station's own website. These are the same kinds of requests your device would make visiting that station's website directly in a browser — the app does not route anything through, or add anything to, those requests beyond what's needed to fetch the stream. As with any network request, the station's server and standard internet infrastructure can see your device's IP address; the app itself does not collect, log, or transmit this information anywhere.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking libraries. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or any other telemetry.

No data sharing

Because the app collects no personal data, there is none to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
